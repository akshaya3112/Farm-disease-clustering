Common Health Patterns

4 Main Livestock Health Patterns

From 43,778 real farm animal records, illness naturally falls into 4 main categories. Spotting the signs early protects your whole herd:

Compare All 4 Patterns →
Pattern 1 (29.2% of Herd)

Senior Cattle Fatigue & Loss of Appetite

🐂

Most common in mature dairy cows and buffalo (average age 12 years). Animals skip their feed (76%) and appear sluggish or depressed (51%), but without burning fevers.

💡 Care Advice: Provide soft, fresh green feed, ensure plenty of clean drinking water, and give them a quiet, shaded resting area.
Pattern 2 (22.2% of Herd)

Severe Breathing Distress & Heavy Sweats

🚨

Heavy visible sweating, fast shallow breathing, and sudden exhaustion (~51% each). This is a serious infectious warning that needs fast isolation.

🚨 Emergency Advice: Move to a separate quarantine pen right away. Do not stress the animal; call your veterinarian immediately.
Pattern 3 (25.1% of Herd)

Young Animal Lameness & Skin Bumps

🐐

Seen mainly in young goats and sheep (average age 2–5 years) with normal body temperatures. Signs include difficulty walking, limping, and skin nodules (42%).

💡 Care Advice: Inspect hooves for stones or foot rot, check skin for ticks or insect bites, and keep bedding clean and dry.
Pattern 4 (23.4% of Herd)

High Fever Crisis & Sudden Weakness

🔥

High body temperature (averaging 103.8°F) across any livestock type, accompanied by sudden loss of appetite (79%) and deep weakness.

⚠️ Care Advice: Cool the animal immediately using damp cloths in a shaded pen. Provide electrolyte water and contact a veterinarian.
