Methodological Principles

Zero Diagnostic Label Leakage

The Kaggle Disease label column was completely isolated prior to any scaling, encoding, or distance computations. It is referenced solely after clustering as an optional descriptive cross-check to observe clinical concordance.

Dual-Metric Distance Geometry

Standard K-Means operates in Euclidean space (ℜ30). Because 24 features are binary symptom indicators, we also built a Manhattan K-Medians model where coordinates are medians, providing an honest comparison of L1 vs L2 geometries.
