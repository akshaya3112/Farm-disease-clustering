Interpretable Rule Heuristics:

  • Profile 1 Rule: Age > 8.5 years → Geriatric cattle complex.
  • Profile 2 Rule: High incidence of diaphoresis (sweats) & fatigue → Acute diaphoretic group.
  • Profile 3 Rule: Low/normal temperature (≤ 102.2 °F) + younger age → Sub-febrile young stock.
  • Profile 4 Rule: Severe hyperthermia (> 103.0 °F) → Acute febrile syndrome.

Note: The surrogate model is strictly an explanatory tool and must not be used as an independent classifier.
